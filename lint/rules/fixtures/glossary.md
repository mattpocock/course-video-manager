# Fixture glossary

The `local/glossary-terms` suite reads this file, laid out as GLOSSARY.md is.

## Language

**Section**:
An ordered group of Lessons. Its **Lesson**s come in order.
_Avoid_: Module, Unit

**CourseVersion**:
A snapshot of a course's structure.
_Avoid_: Version (too vague), Revision

**Discard**:
Throwing away a Pending Version.
_Avoid_: Rollback, Delete version

**Submit**:
Turning the Draft into a Pending Version.
_Avoid_: Freeze, Snapshot

**Preserved Snapshot**:
A snapshot kept from before a restore.
_Avoid_: Backup copy

**Clip Section**:
A divider between Clips.
_Avoid_: Divider, Section

**Marked Ready** / **Marked TODO**:
The author's own call on a Lesson.
_Avoid_: TODO flag

**Layer**:
One thing drawn in a frame.
_Avoid_: Track

**Speaker Footage**:
One speaker's own recording.
_Avoid_: Local recording, Track

**Overlay Template** (not yet implemented):
A saved overlay.
_Avoid_: Preset
